About Techroxx Ecosystem

Techroxx is a comprehensive technology ecosystem dedicated to bridging the gap between academics, industry, innovation, and employability. We empower students, professionals, and organizations through advanced technology solutions, real-world industry projects, specialized events, telemetry hackathons, cloud bootcamps, and professional mentorship.

Techroxx serves as an R&D and corporate consultancy hub, providing engineering services, embedded systems development, PCB design, cloud computing integration, and academic skill development.

Academic Departments & Programs

We offer specialized academic training and curriculum development tracks across multiple engineering and management disciplines, ensuring students gain industry-aligned capabilities:

  • Computing & Software Engineering (CSE): Focusing on software design, web technologies, backend architectures, dynamic web applications, and database management.
  • Electronics & Communications (ECE/EEE): Providing hands-on training in PCB layout design, embedded systems, microcontrollers (ESP32), IoT telemetry, and hardware prototypes.
  • Business Management: Developing leadership, entrepreneurship, project engineering methodologies, and digital portfolio management.

Ecosystem Services & Industry R&D

Techroxx collaborates with corporate partners and educational institutions to offer a wide range of services:

  • Corporate Consultancy: Custom software solutions, business automation, and system integration services.
  • R&D and Hardware Prototyping: Dedicated laboratory space for electronic prototyping, PCB routing, sensor telemetry, and hardware-software integration.
  • Internship Portfolios: Providing students with real-world project portfolios, industry mentorship, and verified career credentials.
